# NHL Forward Paper Protocol — Proposal After the First Benchmark

October 15, 2026. **Draft for review, not accepted release criteria or authorization to enable real-money recommendations.** The owner approved historical testing followed by paper tracking; the following makes the proposed next phase concrete. The first benchmark's 2025–26 weakness and absent market prices remain material blockers.

## Candidate to freeze before collection

- Create a new version with verified team/franchise continuity, including Utah's rename. Preserve the completed v1 benchmark unchanged; its final seasons have now been examined.
- Freeze the feature definitions, fitting/calibration periods, input cutoff policy, model hash and decision rule before the forward evaluation begins. Any material model change starts a separately identified candidate/cohort.
- A team-only model may operate under expected/unconfirmed lineups as agreed. Its probability must not receive an invented goalie adjustment. Show expected/confirmed/unknown status, its real basis and timestamp. A future goalie adjustment needs separate testing.
- Keep all output explicitly research/paper. No production approval flags, real bankroll writes or automatic wager placement.

## Proposed collection and selection rules

Record a forecast for every eligible regular-season fixture with adequate admitted data, including games receiving no bet. Save actual creation time, input/source versions, model version, target market, outcome and collection failures. Never backdate missing forecasts.

For a paper bet, require a matching PlayNow two-way final-winner market, including OT/shootout, with both selections and a source price time no more than five minutes old. Preserve the original price and any actual manual check separately. Expired prices remain expired, and later observations do not repair old records.

Proposed initial selection threshold: **at least 3 percentage points** of model probability above the price's break-even probability, retaining the existing threshold as a research hypothesis rather than optimizing it on the completed test. Use the first qualifying valid observation per fixture/model version, maximum one paper position per fixture, and a flat **one simulated unit**. No minimum pick count; zero plays is a valid result.

Save model forecasts and every relevant price observation even when no play qualifies. For comparison only, normalize the two matched implied probabilities to obtain a clearly labeled margin-adjusted market reference. Paper execution returns use the actual offered price, not that normalized reference. The permitted manual review is a temporary fallback; a new paid source or allowance increase requires a concrete approved proposal.

## First operational checkpoint

Propose **at least 14 calendar days and 100 resolved forecast/price pairs** before assessing the collection workflow. This is an engineering checkpoint, not evidence of a profitable strategy. Suggested checks:

- At least 95% forecast coverage among fixtures declared eligible before outcomes; report raw total coverage and every exclusion separately.
- Zero post-start forecasts entering the pregame cohort, zero stale quotes admitted as fresh, zero duplicate paper positions, and zero paid calls outside admitted limits.
- Complete, reconcilable paper settlement and explicit pending/void/cancelled handling. Zero changes to actual bankroll balances.

If no bets qualify, report that result; do not lower thresholds to populate the ledger. If matched prices are scarce, report the coverage problem and evaluate a bounded collection improvement.

## Proposed later evidence review

Set the primary review policy before starting: no earlier than **60 calendar days, 300 settled qualifying paper positions and eight calendar-week clusters**. These are minimum review floors, not sufficient proof or a promised time to approval. Sparse opportunities can take substantially longer. Review the complete predefined cohort at the scheduled checkpoint; do not stop when a winning streak produces a favorable chart.

Proposed requirements before considering any real-money release:

1. Probability quality and reliability remain acceptable, including recent-period diagnostics; paired scoring comparisons against the contemporaneous market reference support improvement, with a predeclared cluster-based uncertainty method.
2. Flat-unit paper ROI after applicable settlement adjustments is positive, and its predeclared 95% uncertainty interval has a lower bound above zero. No matched odds means no ROI claim. Minimum sample floors alone cannot satisfy this requirement.
3. Results are not driven entirely by one team, a tiny extreme-probability bin or one favorable period. Show the most recent half separately and assess the first benchmark's instability.
4. Source coverage, quote freshness, late lineup changes and operational failures are accounted for, with no hidden exclusion of unsuccessful observations.

Before adoption, the precise scoring/reliability thresholds and uncertainty method need to be locked in the candidate plan, with a power/sensitivity assessment using the actual paper odds. Weekly clusters and small samples have limits; an interval is evidence under its assumptions, not a guarantee of future profit. A failed or inconclusive review keeps the candidate in research. Any continued evaluation or model revision gets a prospectively defined next checkpoint, not a retrospectively chosen winning interval.

Passing research criteria does not automatically deploy or approve the model. Review the evidence and the compatible production contract explicitly. Existing price/risk/spending protections remain in force throughout.
