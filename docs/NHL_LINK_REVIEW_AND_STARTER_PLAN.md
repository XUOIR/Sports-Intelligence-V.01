# NHL link review and starting-goalie plan

Reviewed October 14, 2026, Pacific time. Active repository: XUOIR/Sports-Intelligence-V.01. This is a documentation review before implementation: no accounts, subscriptions, trials, sports-data requests, odds refreshes or AI generations were activated. Public documentation and published schemas were reviewed; actual latency, data completeness and account entitlements were not tested.

## Recommendation

Continue with the existing NHL feed, MoneyPuck advanced statistics, protected PlayNow refreshes and source-backed manual starter confirmation. No newly reviewed low-cost source establishes all of the missing requirements. A reproducible forecast pipeline and genuine chronological validation still require our own work; adding another basic scoreboard API does not complete them.

The [Daily Faceoff public goalie page](https://www.dailyfaceoff.com/starting-goalies) was also inspected: its October 14 page displayed named confirmed goalies, report timestamps and original reporter links. This supports a no-subscription manual verification workflow, not a licensed automated API. This inspection occurred after those scheduled game starts; no confirmation was inserted into an earlier snapshot or represented as having been observed before the game. [MoneyPuck's published downloads](https://moneypuck.com/data.htm) remain available for attributed noncommercial use; different use requires inquiry, and unapproved scraping outside the listed downloads is not permitted.

The owner's suggestion to estimate a starter is useful for a provisional preview. Keep projections separate from factual confirmation and from approved forecasts. Preserve the existing confirmation gate and spending controls. Do not purchase or integrate a new provider on the strength of this review.

## Important access and freshness findings

**SportsDataIO:** The provider's [access guide](https://sportsdata.io/developers) distinguishes its scrambled integration trial from real data. Discovery Lab offers last-season data free, but its current paid products are next-day delayed: Fantasy $99/month, Odds $99/month, combined $149/month, with 100–1,000 daily calls depending on tier. That does not solve current pregame confirmations. Exact free historical NHL endpoint coverage, original announcement revisions/timestamps and retention rights remain untested. Live Leagues access needs a quote. Its [workflow](https://sportsdata.io/developers/workflow-guide/nhl) says projected goalies appear the prior evening and formal confirmations are reflected within minutes of announcements; that is a provider claim, not our measured latency.

**Sportradar:** [Default trials](https://developer.sportradar.com/getting-started/docs/your-account) last 30 days, with 1,000 requests per rolling 30 days and one query/second. A trial is temporary access, not a permanent free production plan. Production quotas/pricing are contractual. No account or trial was started.

**Highlightly:** The [lineup schema](https://highlightly.net/nhl-api/documentation/lineups/) lists player identity, position and `isScratched`; it does not document a starting-goalie confirmation flag. Its examples are declared schema examples, not observed live data. This cannot grant our confirmation gate. The [match list](https://highlightly.net/nhl-api/documentation/matches/) documents one-minute refreshes, [match detail](https://highlightly.net/nhl-api/documentation/matches/by-id/) two minutes, and [player statistics](https://highlightly.net/nhl-api/documentation/players/statistics/) daily season aggregates. None establishes the required rolling xG/GSAx feed. The [odds documentation](https://highlightly.net/nhl-api/documentation/odds/) excludes free access, says prematch updates occur several times daily and live odds every ten minutes; PlayNow coverage and matching settlement rules were not verified. The [terms](https://highlightly.net/terms/) restrict use supporting gambling/betting operations. Clarification for this actual odds/edge product would be needed before adoption. Direct plans block at quota and reset daily at midnight UTC; third-party billing/reset terms can differ.

Polling a one-minute source every thirty seconds cannot make its underlying updates occur twice as often. Even one request each minute for eight hours costs 480 requests/day, exceeding Highlightly's 100/day free allowance before pagination or other endpoints. These are calculated workloads, not tests of source delay. We should not replace the existing official scoreboard with a duplicate subscription.

## Provisional goalie previews

Proposed behavior, not implemented by this research:

1. Identify eligible current-roster goalie candidates. Use recent **starts**, appearances, minutes played, last appearance, team schedule/rest and source-backed availability when available. Games played alone includes relief appearances and cannot stand in for starts.
2. Show `Projected Starter` with the observed facts and an explanation, or `Starter Unknown` when candidates cannot be distinguished. Initial rules are an unvalidated ranking: no invented numeric certainty or automatic factual confirmation.
3. Allow a separate manual `Confirmed Starter` action with the original report/source URL and actual observation time. Keep confirmation and retraction history. A guess cannot prefill a checked confirmation box or overwrite a sourced report.
4. Once a probability model is genuinely validated, show how its forecast changes across plausible goalie pairings. Until then, descriptive comparisons remain available without invented win probabilities, numerical edges or stakes.
5. Evaluate and save the projection's accuracy separately from the game-outcome model. Any future probability-weighted goalie mixture needs calibrated starter probabilities and separate validation for use with unconfirmed goalies. Do not silently reuse a confirmed-starter model as if the uncertainty vanished.

The current `nhl_factors.py` research contract requires source-backed confirmations observed before the T−30 checkpoint, also requiring the source timestamp within the prior thirty minutes; `engine.py` freezes at that checkpoint. An earlier morning report cannot be assigned a fictitiously newer publication time to pass that check. The next implementation needs to distinguish original publication, actual observation and any genuine re-verification, and test the intended treatment of still-valid earlier reports. A report arriving after the checkpoint cannot repair an earlier snapshot. A later-anchor model could be designed and validated separately if needed; this review does not change the anchor or relax the gate.

## The remaining model work

The repository's latest release and current source agree that normalized advanced factors remain research-only. This review did not query production anew. Existing saved prices and descriptive statistics must not be presented as approved model probabilities.

The active validation code partitions chronologically by Pacific game date with embargo dates and requires at least 300 training, 100 calibration and 100 untouched test records after partitioning. Historical game results or actual starters discovered after a game do not establish what was known before it. Reconstructed retrospective research must preserve its real provenance and remain distinguishable from genuine forward forecasts; historical xG models must also be checked for future-data leakage.

Next implementation should complete reproducible inputs and offline chronological evaluation, add the clearly labeled provisional/confirmed starter workflow, and continue genuine forward capture. Approved forecasts and signed edge against fresh, correctly matched PlayNow prices can then feed saved forecasts/results/performance only after the actual validation and verification requirements pass. A new provider subscription does not supply that approval.

Preserve durable request admission, shared caches, hard caps, no retry storms, provider failure cooldowns, source timestamps and the US$3 monthly AI ceiling. No paid fallback or automatic generation is proposed. Cito/CS2 remains deferred while NHL is the priority.
